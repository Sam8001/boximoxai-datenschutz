Frequently asked questions

How do I find an item or a box?

Search for its name on the Home screen. If you have the box in front of you, scan its QR code in the app to see what’s inside.

How do I restore a purchase?

Sign in with the same BoximoxAI account and Apple Account you used for the purchase. Open the in-app Shop and tap ?Restore purchases.?

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings, open Account, and tap ?Delete account.? If you no longer have access to the app, you can request account deletion by email.
